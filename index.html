A Beginners Guide to Badminton


Basic Rules

  • Games are played to 21 points
  • Serves must be hit below the waist

Equipment Required

  • Racket
  • Shuttle
  • Badminton Shoes
  • Badminton Attire

Basic Skills

  • Drop Shot
  • Drive
  • Smash
  • Clear
